01 — Mechanism

How a payment happens

This is the exact scheme of the x402 protocol, adapted for Hedera. A requester's transfer is signed but deliberately left incomplete — Blocky402, the facilitator, adds the missing fee-payer signature and submits it. The requester never touches gas; the facilitator sponsors it.

1

Ask for the resource

A requester agent calls a gated endpoint — say, POST /paid/research/news.

2

402 Payment Required

The researcher replies with exactly what it costs, in tinybars, and who to pay.

3

A partially-signed transfer

The requester builds a Hedera TransferTransaction, signs it with its own key, and leaves the fee-payer slot open.

4

Blocky402 verifies and settles

It checks the transfer is exactly what was asked for, adds its own signature, and submits it to Hedera testnet — paying the network fee itself.

5

The researcher delivers

Once settlement confirms, the real deliverable — generated on the spot — comes back in the response.
